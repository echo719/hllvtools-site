Can your PC run it?

The official spec table hides its biggest caveat in the footnotes: both tiers target only 1080p / 60 fps, and both assume upscaling is on. Check your hardware against the two official anchors below — no third tier invented.

What "minimum" really means

At minimum spec the 1080p/60 target assumes DLSS Ultra Performance — internally the game renders far below 1080p. Even the recommended tier promises 60 fps only at 1080p with upscaling. If you play at 1440p/4K or want high refresh, treat the recommended row as your floor, not your goal.

What the beta showed: 100-player servers hit the CPU

This is a UE5 game where a full server means 100 live humans plus helicopters, boats and tunnels — and the May open beta's performance megathread (102 replies) ran well below what the spec table implies: an RTX 4060 Ti + i5-11600K reported ~60 fps on low in full servers, and a 3080 Ti + i7-12700K saw 55–70 fps on low with only 50–70% GPU usage — the classic signature of being CPU/engine-bound in mass fights. Press coverage echoed the beta's performance complaints. Two honest caveats: the devs said some beta video settings were bugged, and the beta also shipped a 60 Hz refresh-rate lock in the config that made everything feel worse than raw fps. Team17's own pre-launch blog says over 300 bugs were fixed and performance/stability improved across the board since that beta.

Official launch-week performance notes (Team17 blogs, Aug 12–16):
  • AMD GPU instability is a known issue at launch (driver-related). Temporary workarounds Team17 suggests: add -dx12 -USEALLAVAILABLECORES -malloc=system to your Steam launch options (or -dx11 -USEALLAVAILABLECORES -malloc=system if that doesn't help), set in-game graphics to High, and disable the in-game overlay in AMD Software: Adrenalin Edition. Update (Aug 16): Hotfix 1 is confirmed live as of Aug 16, 2026 10:00 UTC and includes further AMD GPU stability improvements per Team17's official notes. Team17 hasn't said whether the manual launch-option workaround above is now redundant, so we're leaving it up until a player confirms it's no longer needed — and we're still seeing scattered fresh AMD crash reports as of Aug 17, so treat this as improved, not necessarily solved. One concrete post-Hotfix-1 example: an RX 7600 owner reported a GPU crash dump on launch with every boot-option combination tried (default, -dx12, and -dx11 all crashed), and a second player replied confirming the same failure on their own system (Aug 16 report thread) — so the launch-option workaround isn't a guaranteed fix for everyone. A second AMD symptom also outlasted the hotfix: white texture flickering/ghosting on the ground, reported at launch on a 7800 XT (a separate Aug 14 report described the same symptom but its GPU model was garbled by a typo, so we're not citing that one as a specific model), and still happening to a 9070 owner roughly six hours after Hotfix 1 shipped; another player in the same thread says disabling the in-game overlay in AMD Software: Adrenalin Edition fixed it for them (flickering report thread, active Aug 14–17).
  • FSR is not available at launch. FSR 4.1 is implemented but still being tested; it's coming in a future update.
  • The dedicated NVIDIA Game Ready Driver was delayed at NVIDIA's end — expected "early next week" as of Aug 14. Use your latest existing driver in the meantime.
  • If you played the Crossplay Playtest on PC, Team17 recommends resetting your video settings to default so they recalibrate correctly for the launch build.
Post-hotfix crash cluster (community reports, Aug 18): a fast-growing "Crashing after hotfixes" thread (29 replies in its first day) collects at least 11 different players reporting crashes that began after the hotfixes — mid-match crash-to-desktop, black screen with no display signal, or freezing on the loading screen — and notably on both vendors: NVIDIA (RTX 5070, 5070 Ti, 5080) and AMD (RX 9070 XT, 7900 XTX). Posters explicitly report that updating drivers and clearing the shader cache did not help; one player mentioned the -dx11 launch option helping them, but that's a single uncorroborated report. In a separate thread, two players posted UE5 crash logs showing the same EXCEPTION_ACCESS_VIOLATION error. Team17's pinned Aug 17 "Server Stability Update" confirms they're aware of new post-Hotfix-1 crash reports and investigating (details on our server status page); the Aug 18 Patch 1.2 preview promises broad stability fixes but names no specific client-crash fix yet.
