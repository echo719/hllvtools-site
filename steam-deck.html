Steam Deck & Linux status CONFIRMED LAUNCH DAY

Verdict: it works. EAC does not block Steam Deck at launch — confirmed by an independent hands-on test the day the game unlocked, corroborated by community Proton reports. Performance is the real catch: playable, but not comfortable on the handheld itself.

What we actually know

  • Deck hands-on, launch day: Steam Deck HQ booted the shipped build, got through the tutorial and a full live match with no EAC block. Performance: even on lowest settings with TSR at 35%, framerate dropped below 30 while walking and hovered around 30 FPS overall — their verdict is "not a game to play on the deck" handheld-first, though they expect it to fare better on Steam Machine at 1080p or lower.
  • Independent corroboration: ProtonDB rates the game Gold (19 reports) — multiple separate Linux/Proton users report it installs and launches "fine out of the box, no tinkering," with EAC authenticating into multiplayer normally. Valve's own Deck Verified badge is still unrated ("Unknown") as of launch day — this is community-tested, not Valve-certified.
  • The anti-cheat is kernel-level Easy Anti-Cheat, and the developer did carry the Proton-enabled switch from beta into launch — the open question from Aug 8 (whether launch EAC config would match the beta) is now resolved: yes, it matches.
  • Performance complaints aren't Deck-exclusive. Separate Steam Discussion reports describe sub-60 FPS stutter on desktop Windows/Linux hardware too (e.g. an RX 9070 XT system reporting 29–42 FPS) — so the Deck's low framerate is likely general launch-day optimization, not a handheld-specific penalty.
If you're buying primarily for Deck: it will run, but expect sub-30 FPS at the lowest settings — treat it as a "docked to a TV" or Steam Machine game rather than a handheld one for now. Steam refunds cover <2h played within 14 days if that's a dealbreaker.

Still open

  • No official Valve "Deck Verified" rating yet — badge status is "Unknown" as of launch day.
  • No developer statement confirming Deck support was an intentional launch target (it works, but appears untuned for it).

Last verified Aug 14, 2026, ~01:30 UTC (launch day, ~9h post-unlock) — Steam Deck HQ hands-on (Aug 13) + ProtonDB aggregate reports (Gold, 19 reports) cross-checked.
