Crossplay & cross-progression, precisely

Yes — PC, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S play together at launch, a series first. But it's server-side, not matchmaking: every server declares its platform policy, and you filter in the browser.

How crossplay actually works

  • Official Dev Q&A: "Server owners will be able to configure which platforms can access their servers, including full crossplay, PC-only or console-only options."
  • Servers are labelled in the browser (cross-platform / PC only / console only) and you can filter by them. Cross-platform squads are supported.
  • There is no input-based matchmaking — the game uses a server browser. Platform isolation exists mainly for input fairness (mouse vs controller); consoles get aim assist.
  • The July 24–27 crossplay playtest already ran Xbox-only, PS5-only and console-crossplay variants live.
  • Last-gen (PS4 / Xbox One): not supported.
