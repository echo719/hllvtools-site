Is the Deluxe Edition worth it?

Short answer: only if you want the skins. The $20 difference is purely cosmetic — no maps, no weapons, no early access, no gameplay content. Two of the three Deluxe packs don't even arrive until later in 2026.

Exact contents

Base game (full)✅✅
Pre-order bonus: US Army Boat Crew Uniform✅ (pre-order)✅ (pre-order)
Field Supply: NVA Boat Crew Uniform—✅ at launch
Field Supply: ANZAC & NVA—🕐 Q3 2026
Field Supply: Green Berets & Navy SEALs—🕐 Q4 2026
Maps / weapons / modes / early accessIdentical — Deluxe adds nothing

Sources: Steam store page · PlayStation Store (Deluxe contents + delivery windows) · Xbox Store

Prices right now (post-launch)

  • The Aug 13 pre-order discount has ended. Standard and Deluxe are both back to full price: $39.99 / $59.99 on Steam.
  • Xbox version is Xbox Play Anywhere: one purchase covers Xbox Series X|S and the Microsoft Store PC version, with shared progression.

Verdict by player type

  • Just want to play at launch: Standard. Every map, weapon and mode is identical.
  • Collector / faction-skin enjoyer: Deluxe is $20 for four uniform packs across the year — fine if that's your thing, but note two packs are IOUs (Q3/Q4 2026).
  • On the fence: buy Standard now (keeps the pre-order skin), upgrade later — the Field Supplies will sell separately as DLC.
Skins are tied to the store you buy on — they do not cross platforms.
