How the solver works

A grid reference resolves to a coordinate: the letter and number name the big square, then each keypad digit narrows it by a factor of three (200 m → 66.7 m → 22.2 m). Range is the straight-line distance between the two points. Azimuth is the bearing from gun to target, given in degrees and in NATO mils — 6,400 to a circle, the scale the Hell Let Loose series has always used for indirect fire.

Once a shell's table is live, the same input maps distance to an elevation setting in degrees (the unit the game's own HUD uses — not mils), interpolating between calibrated sample points rather than a fitted curve we can't defend. Every table on this site ships with the sample count, source, and the date it was measured.
