Renting a server, without the guesswork

Community servers are partner-hosted only — no public server files, no SteamCMD self-hosting. Six companies are on the official partner list, and rentals open around launch.

The official partners

Listed on the game's own server-rentals page: G-Portal · GTXGaming · Low.ms · Streamline Servers · Nitrado · Qonzer. Anyone else selling "HLLV servers" is not on the official list — check before paying.

What server owners can configure

  • Platform access — full crossplay, PC-only, or console-only (official Dev Q&A). Your community's input-fairness policy is a rental-time decision; servers are labelled accordingly in the browser. (How crossplay works →)
  • Admin tooling — the original game has a documented RCON protocol; for Vietnam the devs have hinted at expanded admin tools but published nothing yet. We update this section when partners expose the real panel.
  • Mods — realistically no: UE5 + kernel-level EAC + partner-only hosting leaves no modding surface at launch.
Launch week doesn't require renting anything — official and partner community servers were plentiful in every test window. Rent when your clan needs its own rules, map rotation or region.
