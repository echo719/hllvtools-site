Field manual

Voice chat, explained

Voice in the HLL series is routed by channel, not one big lobby: local proximity, your squad's radio, and a command net for leaders. That design is why the settings menu confuses everyone — and why "open mic" complaints dominated the beta.

The three channels

ChannelBeta default (PC)Who hears you
Proximity (local)VFriendly players physically near you — enemies never hear you
Squad / unit radioCYour ~6-player squad, anywhere on the map
Command radioXSquad leaders + Commander only (leadership role required)

An on-screen indicator lights up per channel when someone transmits, with your own name shown while you broadcast (beta-era community documentation). This channel structure is carried over from the original HLL (HLL1 reference); exact Vietnam launch defaults get re-verified Aug 13.

Muting people & killing the open mic

  • Mute one player: in the original HLL you open the scoreboard (Tab) and click the mute icon by their name — it silences voice and text. HLL1 mechanic — confirming at launch
  • Turn a whole channel down: the series exposes separate volume sliders per chat type in Settings → Audio, so you can lower proximity without touching squad radio. HLL1 mechanic — confirming at launch
  • Your own mic feels always-on? Two beta-confirmed causes: a stuck-transmit bug (a quick tap or overlapping keypress locks the channel open — clear it with a deliberate press-and-release), and controller Layout A, which uses voice activation instead of push-to-talk — switch to Layout C.
  • The transmit keybind is mislabeled. There is no setting called "push to talk" — it hides under Controls as "Radio channel On/Off". Full walkthrough: push to talk "not working".

Console & crossplay voice

On controller you hold LB and pick the channel with the right stick (beta behavior; LB was also the default transmit key in the playtest PTT thread). Cross-platform squads are supported, so squad radio works across PC, PS5 and Xbox on crossplay servers — details on the crossplay page.

When voice just dies

The series runs voice through the third-party Vivox service, and most mid-match failures (a channel going silent, transmit keys ignoring you) are VOIP-connection drops, not keybinds. The beta-era fix order: Settings → Audio → VOIP Disconnect → Reconnect; if that hangs, leave the server and rejoin for a fresh handshake.

Team17-confirmed launch bugs (official, Aug 12–15 blog posts):
  • Proximity chat distortion, caused by EOS. Voices over proximity chat can sound distorted/robotic. Team17 says this is already resolved in Squad and Leadership chat; for proximity chat specifically, their dev-comms thread names EOS (Epic Online Services) as the cause and says they're still working with their VOIP partner on a fix — no ship date yet.
  • Tunnels break the speaker indicator. While travelling through a tunnel, you may not be able to see who's speaking in any VOIP channel — including yourself.
  • Squad Leader leaving kills proximity for the unit. If your Squad Leader leaves the unit, the rest of the squad stops being heard in proximity chat by other players.
  • PS5: audio cutting out for some players — fix incoming. Originally listed as under investigation; Team17's Aug 14 dev-comms post confirms a fix is done and shipping in Hotfix 1, targeted for "this weekend."
  • Directional audio — newly flagged, under investigation. Team17's dev-comms thread names "directional audio" as a distinct issue they're actively working to address, separate from the items above — no further detail on the symptom or a fix date yet.
This matches the intermittent voice failures the community was independently reporting on Steam and Reddit (see the PTT page) — now with an official acknowledgment and a fix in progress rather than a mystery.
