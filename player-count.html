How many people are actually playing?

Team17 doesn't publish a player-count API, and we tested pulling Steam's numbers straight into this page ourselves — blocked by CORS, confirmed on launch day. So instead of faking a live widget, this page gives you the numbers that are actually true right now, plus the exact links to check the real live count yourself.

Is there a live player-count number?

Not on this page, and not honestly anywhere third-party — Valve doesn't expose a public per-game concurrent-player API that a static site can call directly from the browser; we confirmed the direct fetch fails with a CORS error rather than pretend it works. The two places that actually track this live, straight from Steam's own data:

  • SteamCharts — dedicated concurrent-player history and graph for Hell Let Loose: Vietnam specifically.
  • Steam's own "Most Played" chart — Valve's official live ranking of the top 100 games by current players.

Where Vietnam stood at our last check

Snapshot only — treat this as "last time we looked," not live. Four checks so far, close enough together to show the trend rather than just one point in time — and spread across different hours of the day, since concurrent players swing a lot with the clock:

  • Aug 14, 2026 ~20:15 UTC (≈28h post-launch): #1 on Steam's live Top Sellers chart (by revenue), ahead of Marvel Rivals (#2) and Counter-Strike 2 (#3). On the Most Played chart (by concurrent players): #73, tagged "NEW," with 22,741 concurrent players and a same-day peak of 30,267.
  • Aug 15, 2026 ~08:20 UTC (≈40h post-launch): #2 on Top Sellers — Counter-Strike 2 moved into #1, Vietnam still holds a top-3 spot. On Most Played, it had dropped out of the Top 100 entirely. SteamCharts' own per-game page was returning a server error at check time, so we don't have an exact concurrent number for this snapshot — flagging the outage rather than guessing a figure.
  • Aug 17, 2026 ~01:20 UTC (≈81h post-launch): #3 on Top Sellers — still a top-3 revenue spot four days out. Back inside the Most Played Top 100 at #54, tagged "NEW," with 24,122 concurrent players and a same-day peak of 27,855 (read directly off Steam's own chart). SteamCharts corroborates with a close independent number: 23,521 players an hour before this check, 27,630 24-hour peak.
  • Aug 17, 2026 ~20:20 UTC (≈100h post-launch): #8 on Top Sellers — down from top-3 but still inside the top 10 on revenue four days out. On Most Played: #85, with 16,970 concurrent players and a same-day peak of 27,693 (read directly off Steam's own chart). SteamCharts corroborates: 17,053 players an hour before this check, 27,630 for both the 24-hour and all-time peak. This check lands close to the same hour as the very first one three days earlier (Aug 14 ~20:15 UTC, 22,741 concurrent) — a same-time-of-day comparison, which removes the daily up-and-down swing as a factor and points to a real decline over the launch week rather than just a different point in the daily cycle.
  • For scale, at the first check #1 on Most Played was Counter-Strike 2 at just over 1M concurrent. The closest 50v50-scale tactical-shooter comparison, Rising Storm 2: Vietnam, has averaged only ~320 concurrent players over the last 30 days on SteamCharts.

Sources: Steam Most Played chart · Steam Top Sellers chart · SteamCharts (Hell Let Loose: Vietnam) · SteamCharts (Rising Storm 2: Vietnam) — checked live via browser at all four timestamps above.

How big was the launch-day spike?

Team17's own words, from their Aug 14 launch-day blog: "We've already seen almost three times the number of concurrent players compared to Hell Let Loose's all-time peak." That's an official comparison, not a number — but independent tracking reported by TechTimes (citing ThePCEnthusiast) put Aug 13 launch-day concurrent Steam players above 30,000, alongside beta context of 352,000 peak participants and a beta daily-active-user high of 184,000.

Sources: official "Important Launch Info" blog (Aug 14) · TechTimes launch-quality report (Aug 14)
